Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Machine Learning is a branch of AI that allows computers to learn patterns from data and make predictions without being explicitly programmed.

Deep Learning uses artificial neural networks with multiple layers to learn complex patterns from large amounts of data.

Natural Language Processing (NLP) allows computers to understand, process, and generate human language.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines information retrieval with language generation. It allows AI systems to search external knowledge sources and use retrieved information to generate better answers.
